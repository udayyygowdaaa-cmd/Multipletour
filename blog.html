Why we listen, what we ask, and how you can tell us how your trip went.

Mysore in a day: a simple plan

Palaces, a hill temple and a garden evening, all within easy reach.

Read the full post

Mysore is compact, so one full day is enough for the highlights. Start early with the Mysore Palace, one of the most visited monuments in India. Check the visiting hours before you go, because they can change on festival days.

Late morning, drive up Chamundi Hills for the temple and the view over the city. Lunch can be a simple local meal back in town.

In the afternoon visit the Jaganmohan Palace or the local markets for silk, sandalwood and sweets. Finish with an evening at Brindavan Gardens near the Krishnaraja Sagara dam.

The best months are generally October to March. Dasara season is beautiful but busy, so book early.

Coorg: when to go and what to see

Waterfalls, coffee estates and misty hills, a favourite weekend drive.

Read the full post

Coorg (Kodagu) is a green hill region famous for coffee estates, waterfalls and cool weather. It is an easy weekend trip from Bangalore and Mysore.

When to go: October to March is comfortable and clear. The monsoon turns everything lush and green, though some viewpoints may be misty and roads wet.

What to see: Abbey Falls, a walk through a coffee estate, the Dubare elephant camp, Raja Seat for sunsets and the Namdroling Monastery at Bylakuppe.

Tip: carry a light jacket, comfortable shoes and, in the rains, a raincoat. Start early to enjoy the roads.

Choosing the right vehicle for your group

Sedan, SUV, Tempo Traveller or bus: how to pick without overpaying.

Read the full post

Up to 4 people: a hatchback or sedan is the most economical choice for couples and small families.

5 to 7 people: an SUV or MUV gives more seats and luggage room, which matters on longer trips.

10 to 17 people: a Tempo Traveller keeps the group together and is popular for family functions, pilgrimages and group tours.

Larger groups: a bus suits schools, colleges and corporate outings.

Tell us your group size, luggage and route, and we will suggest the right vehicle with a clear quote. The exact make and model depend on what is available on your date.

Hill station packing checklist

What to carry for Ooty, Coorg, Munnar and Kashmir.

Read the full post

Documents: a photo ID for every traveller, your booking details and some cash for small shops.

Clothes: light woollens or a jacket (nights are cool even in summer), comfortable walking shoes and a raincoat or umbrella.

Health: your regular medicines, motion-sickness tablets for winding roads, sunscreen and a small first-aid kit.

Gadgets: a power bank, charger and a camera or phone with free storage.

Extras: a reusable water bottle, snacks for the road and a small bag for the day trips.
